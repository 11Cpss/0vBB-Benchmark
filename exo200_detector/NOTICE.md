# EXO-200 workflow notice

The `exobench` workflow and frozen `energybench` evaluator originated in the
collaborative Rare AI Lab benchmarking effort and are included in this public
repository with collaborator permission. Their inclusion is intended to keep
Transformer and specialized-model comparisons on the same data processing,
split, training, checkpoint-selection, and evaluation protocol.

The frozen evaluator snapshot was copied on 2026-08-31 from the collaborator's
lab checkout at `summer/src/energybench`; exact provenance and hashes are in
`frozen_energybench/SOURCE.json`. The repository does not claim authorship of
collaborator-contributed portions.

EXO-200 detector data are separate third-party material and are not included.
Version 1 is licensed CC BY 4.0 by the EXO-200 Collaboration and is available at
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20419164.
